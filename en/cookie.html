What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites send to the user's browser, where they are stored to be transmitted back to the same websites on subsequent visits. Cookies are used for various purposes and may have different characteristics.

Cookies Used by This Website

Technical Cookies (Necessary)

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the website and cannot be disabled. They do not collect personally identifiable information.

Name Purpose Duration
baff_cookie_consent Stores the user's cookie consent preference 365 days

Analytics Cookies (Subject to Consent)

These cookies allow us to collect anonymous information about website usage (pages visited, time spent, referral source) in order to improve content and browsing experience. They are only activated with the user's consent.

Third-Party Cookies

The website may integrate content and services from third parties (e.g., Google Fonts, YouTube, social media) that may install their own cookies. The B.A. Film Festival has no control over third-party cookies. For more information, please refer to the privacy policies of the respective services.

How to Manage Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through:

  • The cookie banner: on your first visit, the banner allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Browser settings: all major browsers allow you to manage, block, or delete cookies. Below are links to guides for the most common browsers:
  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: disabling technical cookies may impair the functioning of the website.
