Accreditations

The B.A. Film Festival offers accreditations for film industry professionals, journalists and cinema students. Accreditation provides priority access to screenings, special events and areas reserved for press and industry throughout the entire duration of the festival.

Press

Reserved for journalists, film critics, bloggers and media operators with proven activity in cultural and film journalism. Press accreditation includes access to all screenings, the dedicated press room, press conferences and preview promotional materials.

Request accreditation

Industry

Dedicated to distributors, producers, sales agents, festival programmers and film industry professionals. Industry accreditation offers full access to the programme, networking sessions, one-to-one meetings and the festival's industry area.

Request accreditation

Film students

Open to students enrolled in film schools, universities with degree courses in film and audiovisual disciplines, and fine arts academies with a cinema focus. Student accreditation includes access to screenings, masterclasses and training workshops at the festival.

Request accreditation

How to reach us

By car

A8 Milan–Varese motorway, Busto Arsizio exit. Follow signs for the city centre. Cinema Manzoni is at Via Magenta 70, just minutes from the motorway exit. Nearby parking: Piazza Trento e Trieste car park (5 min walk) and Via Dandolo car park (3 min walk).

By train

Busto Arsizio FN Station, Milan–Varese railway line (Trenord). Frequent trains from Milan Cadorna Station with a journey time of approximately 40 minutes. From the station, Cinema Manzoni is a 10-minute walk along Via Magenta.

Walking from the station

From Busto Arsizio FN Station, exit and walk along Via Magenta towards the centre. Cinema Manzoni is at number 70, reachable in approximately 10 minutes on foot. The route is entirely flat and well-signposted.
