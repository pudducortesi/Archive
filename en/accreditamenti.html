Who can request accreditation

The B.A. Film Festival offers accreditations for film industry professionals, journalists and cinema students. Accreditation provides priority access to screenings, special events and areas reserved for press and industry throughout the entire duration of the festival.

Press

Reserved for journalists, film critics, bloggers and media operators with proven activity in cultural and film journalism.

Industry

Dedicated to distributors, producers, sales agents, festival programmers and film industry professionals.

Film students

Open to students enrolled in film schools, universities with degree courses in film and audiovisual disciplines.
