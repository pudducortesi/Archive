Official Selection

Official Selection of the XXIV edition of the B.A. Film Festival announced

The titles that will make up the Official Selection of BAFF 2026 have been revealed: twelve Italian feature films and ten international works will compete for the prizes of the XXIV edition. A selection that confirms the festival's dedication to auteur cinema attentive to reality and capable of moving audiences. The commission reviewed over four hundred candidate works, selecting the films that best represent new trends in contemporary cinema.
