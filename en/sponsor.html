Friends

The partners who make the festival possible

Home / Friends

The B.A. Film Festival is made possible thanks to the support of companies, organisations and institutions that share our passion for cinema and culture.

Every year our Friends help bring great cinema to Busto Arsizio, supporting screenings, events, masterclasses and the festival's educational activities. Thanks to them, we can continue to offer a world-class programme accessible to all.
