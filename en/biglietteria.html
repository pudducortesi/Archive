Discounts

The B.A. Film Festival offers reduced rates to make cinema accessible to all. The following categories are entitled to reduced rates:

  • Under 18 — Young people under 18 years of age (with valid ID)
  • Over 65 — Adults aged 65 or over (with valid ID)
  • University students — With a valid university student card
  • Carta Giovani holders — With a valid Carta Giovani Nazionale

Discounts apply to both single admissions and festival passes. Discounts cannot be combined. Proof of eligibility must be presented at the venue entrance.

Useful information

Opening hours

The Cinema Manzoni box office is open every day of the festival, from 21 to 28 March 2026, from 3:00 PM to 10:00 PM. The online box office is available 24/7. Online sales close 1 hour before the start of each screening.

Cancellation policy

Tickets purchased online can be refunded up to 48 hours before the start of the screening. Refunds will be made using the same payment method used for the purchase. Festival passes can be fully refunded up to 7 days before the start of the festival. No refunds are available for tickets purchased at the physical box office.

Accessibility

Cinema Manzoni is fully accessible to people with mobility disabilities. Reserved seating is available for wheelchair users and their companions. Companions of certified disabled persons are entitled to free admission. For specific needs, please contact us in advance at info@bafilmfestival.it.

Frequently asked questions

Children up to 5 years of age enter free when accompanied by an adult with a valid ticket. Young people aged 6 to 17 are entitled to the reduced rate of €5 for single admission and €60 for the festival pass, upon presentation of valid ID.

Date or time changes are only possible for tickets purchased online, up to 24 hours before the original screening. Changes are subject to seat availability for the new date or time selected. To make a change, access your personal area on the festival website or contact the box office at info@bafilmfestival.it.

The festival pass (€80 full price, €60 reduced) provides access to all screenings, special events, masterclasses and guest meetings for the entire duration of the festival, from 21 to 28 March 2026.
