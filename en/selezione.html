Official Selection

Films in competition — XXIV Edition

The Official Selection of the XXIV edition of the B.A. Film Festival represents the beating heart of the event: a journey through the most significant voices of contemporary Italian and international cinema.

The selection committee, composed of critics, programmers and industry professionals, reviewed over 1,200 works from 45 countries. From this work of research and analysis emerged the 12 titles in competition, divided into the Italian Competition and International Competition sections, as well as the 4 films of the special retrospective The First Steps of the Masters.

Each selected film carries a unique vision of the world, a distinctive cinematic language and a story capable of questioning and moving. From debuts to works by established directors, the Official Selection of BAFF 2026 invites the audience to discover the cinema of tomorrow.

How to submit a film

The B.A. Film Festival accepts submissions for the XXIV edition throughout the year. Feature films, short films and documentaries completed after 1 January 2025 are eligible. Works must be submitted in their original version with Italian or English subtitles.

Submission is exclusively through the FilmFreeway platform. The deadline for submissions to the next edition is 30 November 2026.
