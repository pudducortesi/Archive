Guests of the XXIV edition

Guest of honour

Director

Marco Ferretti

Marco Ferretti is one of the most acclaimed Italian directors of his generation. Born in Rome in 1962, he made his directorial debut in 1991 with the feature film La luce di settembre, which earned him the Critics' Prize at the Venice Film Festival. Over a thirty-year career he has directed fifteen films, receiving recognition at Cannes, Berlin and Toronto. His intimate and poetic cinema explores family bonds and the transformation of the Italian landscape with a gaze that is both rigorous and compassionate. Among his most celebrated works are Vento di pianura (2003), Il confine dell'acqua (2012) and the recent Cenere viva (2025), which had its world premiere at BAFF.
